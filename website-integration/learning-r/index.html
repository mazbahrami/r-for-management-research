Approach

Learn the research workflow, not isolated commands

The resource is designed for students who are new to R. Each module begins with a management-research question, explains the method in plain language, develops the analysis through editable code, and ends with interpretation, practice, and common mistakes.

The goal is not to memorize syntax. It is to understand how a research question becomes a transparent empirical analysis—and what the evidence can and cannot establish.

Learning Pathway

From raw data to a defensible result

Nine compact modules connect RStudio skills with the methods commonly used in quantitative management research.

Modules

A focused beginner curriculum

Use the modules in sequence or select the topics required for a particular methods course.

01

First Steps in R

RStudio, projects, objects, functions, scripts, and data frames.

02

Clean and Describe

Importing, recoding, missing values, summaries, and visual exploration.

03

Correlations and t-tests

Relationships, group differences, confidence intervals, and effect sizes.

04

Reliability and Scales

Reverse coding, Cronbach's alpha, item checks, and composite scores.

05

EFA and Chi-square

Exploratory measurement structure and categorical association.

06

Linear Regression

Coefficients, model fit, assumptions, diagnostics, and influential cases.

07

Moderation and Mediation

Interactions, indirect paths, visualization, and causal caution.

08

Logistic Regression

Binary outcomes, odds ratios, predicted probabilities, and governance.

09

Replication and Reporting

Comparing samples, preserving outputs, and writing bounded conclusions.

Cases and Data

Management examples that connect across methods

The main data are simulated and transparently documented, allowing unrestricted classroom reuse without privacy risks.

Employee Survey

Leadership, engagement, role clarity, workload, support, burnout, satisfaction, performance, and turnover intention.

Marketing Mix

Weekly sales, advertising channels, discounts, market conditions, and an intentionally influential observation.

Hotel Upgrades

A binary service outcome used to explain odds ratios, predicted probabilities, and responsible operational use.
