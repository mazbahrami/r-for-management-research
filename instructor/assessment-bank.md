# Assessment and question bank

## Exit-ticket prompts

1. What does one row represent in today's dataset?
2. Which variable is the outcome, and what type is it?
3. Write one defensible result sentence.
4. Name one assumption or limitation.
5. What code line would you change to answer a related question?

## Short conceptual questions

1. Why should a graph usually precede a statistical test?
2. What is the difference between an independent and paired t-test?
3. Why must a negatively worded survey item be reverse-coded?
4. What does Cronbach's alpha assess, and what does it not prove?
5. Why use an oblique rotation in an EFA of management constructs?
6. What does a regression coefficient mean when other predictors are included?
7. Why is an influential observation not automatically an error?
8. How does moderation differ from mediation?
9. Why can odds ratios be difficult to communicate?
10. What is the difference between reproduction and replication?

## Applied mini-assignment

Using one supplied dataset, students submit:

- a focused research question;
- a reproducible script or Quarto report;
- one descriptive table or figure;
- one justified statistical analysis;
- a short interpretation with uncertainty;
- one diagnostic or robustness check;
- a limitations paragraph.

## Suggested rubric (20 points)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Research question and method alignment | 4 |
| Reproducible data preparation | 4 |
| Correct analysis | 4 |
| Interpretation and uncertainty | 4 |
| Diagnostics, limitations, and presentation | 4 |
